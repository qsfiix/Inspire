# Marketing to Product Entry Flow

## Flow

1. Home communicates problem and product promise.
2. User sees how profile, data and next steps connect.
3. Primary CTA starts onboarding or guest exploration.
4. Secondary CTA opens About/trust context.
5. If login appears, the page explains the benefit and preserves intent.
6. After entry, first screen echoes the selected intent instead of restarting from zero.

## Anti-pattern

Do not make the public page grand and vague, then force the user into an unrelated technical form. The promise and first product screen must connect.
