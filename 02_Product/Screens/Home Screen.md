# Home Screen

## Goal

Объяснить, что Inspirella помогает разобраться в образовательных и карьерных возможностях через персонализацию, качественные данные и ясную структуру.

## Sections

1. Hero: one clear promise and primary CTA.
2. Why Inspirella: complexity → clarity.
3. How it works: profile → discovery → path → next step.
4. Switzerland focus: initial depth, not a claim of global completeness.
5. Trust/data note: official sources and evolving coverage.
6. Secondary CTA: explore as guest / learn more.
7. Footer with contact and language.

## Tone

Ambitious but concrete. Hero slogan (since 2026-10-12): «Keine Sackgassen. Nur nächste Schritte.» / “No dead ends. Only next steps.” (based on the system's permeability, Durchlässigkeit). The earlier «Нет ничего невозможного, и ты это увидишь» (“Nothing is impossible, and you'll see it”) is kept as the emotional closing line above the final CTA. Page copy must still explain the actual product outcome.

## States

The public home should not depend on database availability to render its core message. External/data blocks have graceful fallback.
