# Decision Support UX

## Decision sequence

1. Name the decision context.
2. Show a small set of options.
3. Explain why each appears.
4. Surface meaningful differences.
5. Show source/quality and what still needs checking.
6. Offer one next step.
7. Let the user save, compare or refine preferences.

## Avoid

- one «best» option without alternatives;
- unexplained scores;
- long AI essay before concrete options;
- hiding uncertainty below the fold;
- forcing a decision when profile is incomplete.

## Microcopy

Use «может подойти», «похоже на совпадение по…», «проверьте…», «альтернативный маршрут». Explain that recommendations support exploration, not admission decisions.
