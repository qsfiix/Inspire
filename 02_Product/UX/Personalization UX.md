# Personalization UX

## Personalization signals

Use explicit inputs first: interests, goals, stage, language and region. Later signals require transparency and consent.

## Explainability pattern

«Мы показали это, потому что…» with 2–4 concrete reasons. Provide «изменить preferences» and «показать другие варианты» actions.

## User control

- edit profile;
- reset or broaden filters;
- save/ignore recommendations;
- correct wrong assumptions;
- understand what is stored.

## Emotional design

Users may arrive uncertain or anxious. Avoid language that closes doors too early. Present routes as possibilities and alternatives, including academic and vocational pathways where relevant.

## Long-term direction

Personalization should become deeper over years, but every layer must improve clarity and agency rather than maximize data collection.
